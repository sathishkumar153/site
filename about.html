Slow Extraction Methodology

The Art of Slow Botanical Distillation

Modern industrial cosmetics prioritize speed and synthetic emulsifiers. At PRONOV, we hold true to the ancient practice of slow infusion. Wild neem, tulsi, amla, and fresh aloe vera undergo 21 days of gradual solar steeping and hand stone-grinding.

By cold-pressing without excessive pressure and never exceeding human body temperature (38°C), our extracts retain their living molecular integrity, raw polyphenols, and cellular nourishment.
