Formulation Science

The Chemistry of Cold-Process Alchemy vs. Industrial Detergent Bars

Conventional commercial soap is mass-boiled, stripped of natural botanical glycerin to sell separately, and padded with synthetic petroleum foaming agents. PRONOV cold saponification takes the opposite path.

01

Step I • Low Temperature

Room Temperature Saponification

Zero external heat is applied during the fusion of virgin fats and plant lye. This shields fragile thermolabile bio-actives in wild neem, holy basil, and saffron from thermal degradation.

Intact antioxidant carotenoids & plant polyphenols.

02

Step II • Atmospheric Alchemy

42-Day Cedar Rack Cure

Patience replaces chemical drying accelerators. Over six weeks on aromatic cedar battens, gentle moisture evaporation hardens each bar while concentrating 100% of native plant glycerin inside the lipid matrix.

Velvety humectant lather that never strips lipid barriers.

03

Step III • Chemical Purity

Zero Artificial Hardening Agents

Free from synthetic PEG, industrial SLS detergents, paraffin fillers, or tallow animal derivatives. The resulting bar dissolves cleanly into micro-bubbles that rinse away without leave-behind synthetic films.

100% biodegradable and waterway-safe runoff.

Preservation Protocol

The Longevity Ritual: How to Extend the Life of Your Cold-Cured Bar.

Because PRONOV bars contain high concentrations of native glycerin and zero chemical hardening polymers, they drink ambient moisture naturally. Following two simple rules will double the lifespan of each artisanal block:

Never rest your bar in pooling puddle dishes. House it on a slotted ceramic or oiled teakwood draining soap deck away from direct shower spray.

Allowing the bar to dry completely between bathing uses keeps the vegetable butter matrix dense and firm, yielding up to 50 fragrant washes per bar.
